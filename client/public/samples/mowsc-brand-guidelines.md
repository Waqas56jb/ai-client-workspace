# MOWSC Brand Guidelines 2026

_Sample reference document bundled with the StudioFlow AI demo._

## Logo
- Use the navy logo on white or light backgrounds.
- Use the white logo on navy or on photography with a navy overlay (min. 60% opacity).
- Keep clear space equal to the logo height on all sides.

## Colours
| Name       | Hex      | Use                                   |
|------------|----------|---------------------------------------|
| Navy       | #0B3D91  | Primary backgrounds, headings         |
| Aqua       | #1FB6C9  | Highlights only — never full fills    |
| Sun Yellow | #FFC531  | Calls to action                       |
| Mist       | #F4FAFC  | Light backgrounds                     |

## Typography
- Headings: Montserrat ExtraBold
- Body: Open Sans

## Voice
Energetic, inclusive and community-first. Celebrate volunteers and juniors.
Always include safety-briefing times in event communications.

## Cup 2026
- Dates: 14–15 March 2026
- Major sponsor: Bayline Marine — logo bottom-right on every Cup asset.
